Privacy policy.

How information is handled when you visit this website or contact us, and the intended scope of our planned internal advertising tool.

1. Who we are

This website presents IQQI, a fragrance brand operated by Shenzhen Laorenyuhai Technology Co., Ltd. Our Chinese legal name is 深圳市老人与海科技有限公司.

For questions about this policy, contact 244566972qq@gmail.com.

2. Website visits and hosting

This is a static information website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no registration, login, online forms, payments, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Our website code does not set cookies or use browser storage. The site uses fonts available on your device and locally hosted styles, without third-party font services.

GitHub, as the hosting provider, logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without signing in to GitHub. These hosting records are distinct from the advertising data described below. GitHub handles hosting data under its own Privacy Statement. You can also read GitHub’s Pages data collection information.

We do not operate a separate visitor database or receive website form submissions. Links to other websites are subject to those websites’ own privacy practices.

3. Email enquiries

Clicking our email link opens your email application. It does not submit a message through this website. If you send an email, your email address, message, attachments and other information you choose to include are processed through the email services used by you and us to deliver and respond to your enquiry.

Our published contact address uses Gmail. Google’s handling of information through that service is explained in its Privacy Policy. We use the information you send to handle your enquiry and related correspondence. Please do not send passwords, API credentials or advertising reports through this contact channel.

4. Planned internal advertising tool

We plan to develop a tool that retrieves reporting data from our own Amazon advertising accounts through the Amazon Ads API. It is intended for internal, read-only analysis of spend, clicks, advertising-attributed sales and ACOS.

The tool has not been connected to the Amazon Ads API. This website does not request Amazon account access, collect advertising reports or connect to Amazon APIs. There is no advertising data processing by the planned tool at this stage.

The planned tool will not change budgets, bids or campaign settings and is not intended to provide advertising management services to third parties. Its data storage, access controls, retention periods and deletion procedures have yet to be implemented. These arrangements and the applicable privacy information need to be established before it begins processing API data.

5. Retention and deletion

Hosting records. GitHub manages retention and deletion of the records it collects as the hosting provider. We do not set GitHub’s retention periods or control deletion of its logs. Requests concerning GitHub’s records should follow the contact options in its Privacy Statement.

Email correspondence. This static website does not set an automatic deletion period for email messages. Correspondence is held in the email services used to send and receive it, rather than in a website database. To request access to, correction of or deletion of information you sent us, email the contact address below. A request may require verification of your identity, and any applicable recordkeeping requirements must be considered. Deletion from a mailbox may not immediately remove copies retained by an email provider or a recipient.

Planned API data. The planned tool currently collects and retains no Amazon Ads API data, so there is no existing tool dataset to delete. This policy does not claim that a future retention schedule or deletion system is already in place.

6. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. It contains public company information and has no login system or database. Its public source files contain no API keys, account credentials or advertising reports.

Email security and storage also depend on the email providers involved. No website or communication service can guarantee absolute security. We do not claim that security controls for the planned internal tool are already implemented.

7. Contact and updates

Privacy enquiries and requests can be sent to 244566972qq@gmail.com, addressed to Shenzhen Laorenyuhai Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市老人与海科技有限公司).

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised update date. The information will need to be reviewed before any internal tool is connected to the Amazon Ads API.
